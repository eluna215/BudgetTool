Everything a paycheck budget needs

Built for people paid weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly, and for anyone who wants to see one paycheck at a time.

🗓️

Pay-period view

Each period runs from payday to the day before the next one, with every bill that falls inside it.

➕

Running "Together" total

Left over from every period adds up, so you can see whether you're getting ahead or falling behind.

⚠️

Early warnings

The dashboard flags every week, month and year where your running total goes negative.

↪️

Drag to move a bill

Paying something late or early? Drag it to another day in the calendar, or change just that one payment.

🎨

Your categories, your colors

Sort payments into Bill, Entertainment, W2L or Other, and income into Job, Side or Other. Pick a color for each.

📈

Charts and a full year

Line charts, month cards and a pay-period table for the whole year, with category totals everywhere.

Set up in three steps

It takes about five minutes.

Enter your pay

Your take-home pay, how often you're paid, and the starting day. Every calculation counts from that day.

Add your bills

Rent, phone, subscriptions, loans. Set how often each repeats and, if it ends, when.

Log spending as you go

Click a day in the calendar to log what you spent. Left over and Together update right away.

Works with how you're paid

Pick one and the whole tool, including the views, totals and charts, follows it.
